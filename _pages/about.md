I am a robotics researcher specializing in **3D perception, multi-modal sensor fusion, localization, neural scene representations, and Vision-Language Navigation**. My PhD research at the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/) (2022–2026) focused on multi-modal robotic perception, 3D computer vision, and neural rendering.

I have hands-on experience developing LiDAR–RGB systems, 6-DoF localization, SLAM, 3D Gaussian Splatting, nonlinear optimization, and real-world robotic perception. My work has been published at venues including ICRA, IROS, RA-L, ISPRS, and CRV, and I also have industry experience in autonomous driving and robotic perception.

Research interests
------
* **3D mapping and reconstruction:** LiDAR-guided 3D Gaussian Splatting, dense SLAM priors, and metric, high-fidelity scene reconstruction
* **Localization and optimization:** 6-DoF visual localization and pose refinement with 3D Gaussian Splatting, feature matching, pose graph optimization, and bundle adjustment
* **Multi-sensor perception:** calibration, registration, and fusion of LiDAR, cameras, IMU, and GNSS, including hardware-triggered sensor synchronization and custom handheld and vehicle-based scanner platforms
* **Vision-Language Navigation and VLA:** view-invariant learning and grounding analysis for navigation agents

Experience
------
* **Graduate Student**, CViSS, University of Waterloo (2023–2026): localization, 3D mapping, and reconstruction
* **SLAM/3D Algorithm Engineer**, Horizon Robotics, Autonomous Driving Department (2021): localization and high-definition mapping
* **Software Development Intern**, Leizhi Technology Inc. (2021): 3D point cloud processing
* **Research Intern**, Tsinghua University IVip Laboratory (2019–2020): multi-sensor SLAM
